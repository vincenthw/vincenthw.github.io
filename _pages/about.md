I am prospective PhD student interested in computer security and privacy, critical data studies, surveillance studies, and better understanding the world that we are creating as AI develops

# Research Interests 
I am interested in exploring and contributing to the fields of (i) Usable security, privacy, and safety– how individuals cybersecurity, safety, and privacy are changing due to the rise of AI agents and methods for self-protection and resistance to AI and ML-powered invasion of privacy and security. (ii) Responsible and trustworthy computing– exploring how to detect and diminish bias in AI and algorithmic systems, how technology might be used for harm mitigation, and ensuring explainable systems. (iii) Critical data studies– the non-neutrality of data and algorithms and the power dynamic this creates, such as how surveillance is being integrated into everyday life, and supercharged by AI, and how this is already impacting vulnerable members of society.
